⚙️
Before you start
  • Use a separate “burner” number for mass-sending — not your main number. Replace it every 7–14 days.
  • One WhatsApp number sends max ~50/day. Push too hard and the daily limit keeps dropping, so send in small batches.
  • Keep your main number for replies & sending your portfolio.
1
Send the first opener

From the system, send your first message to the lead.

💡 Start your cold reach after 3pm — reply rates are higher later in the day.

2
They reply → send case study + portfolio

Switch to your main number. Send Template A with your portfolio. Send the portfolio as a PDF under 5MB — don’t spam separate images.

3
Day 3, no reply → follow up

Send Template B to gently follow up.

4
Portfolio sent, no reply → one more nudge

Ask if they have upcoming events or design-upgrade needs.

5
Quote sent, no reply → offer a discount

Send a limited offer, e.g. -35% new-client campaign, to create urgency.

💡 Quotes under RM5,000 tend to close more easily — adjust the price by the service / scope.

6
Still no reply → move on

Stop here. Anyone who replies can usually be closed.

7
Portfolio doesn’t match their industry?

Ask the client for a reference style, or offer to try one design so they can see what you can do.

8
Track every send

Mark each lead Sent after sending and Replied when they reply — clean stats, and you never double-send.

Message templates

A · Case study — send WITH portfolio when they reply

Here’s a case study to share, perhaps they don’t feature your industry or preferred style. No worries; if you share some reference styles, we can offer a free trial for one post design so you can see our design skills. We can start with smaller request 🤝

B · Follow-up — Day 3, no reply

Hi, just following up here. Not sure if my earlier message reached the right person. If you are not looking for design or marketing support right now, no worries. I can also just send a few relevant sample works for your reference, and you can keep us in mind for future visuals, social media content, packaging, menu, campaign or website support.

📋 How your lead list is built

Each batch (~100 leads) across all Malaysia, focused on visual-driven retail SMEs most likely to need branding, graphic, web design, marketing & more.

  • Beauty / skincare / cosmetics~25%
  • Home / living / decor~20%
  • Fashion / apparel / boutique~18%
  • Maternity / baby / kids~15%
  • Jewelry / watches / accessories~15%
  • Events / wedding / gifting~7%
  • Florists / flower shops~34%
  • Cake shops / bakeries~33%
  • Balloon / party shops~33%

Plus a small mix of café/bakery, florist, electronics, pet & fitness. Every lead is a WhatsApp-ready mobile number, deduped — you won't get a business that was recently collected by anyone. We skip clinics, salons, workshops, auto & industrial.
